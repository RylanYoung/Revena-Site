One product

We do not split solar and battery, because buyers do not.

Every lead tells you whether they want panels, storage or both. That is a field on the lead, not a separate product you have to buy twice.

Roughly speaking, the market splits three ways: households after panels only, households after storage only, and households who want the lot. The third group carries the biggest job value, and the second group is the warmest, because they have already bought solar once and are coming back for the other half.

A battery-only enquiry is somebody with panels on the roof, a bill that still stings and usually a specific trigger behind it. An outage. A feed-in tariff cut. A neighbour who put one in. They are not asking whether storage works. They are asking who is installing it.

Questions

Before you ask

Do you sell battery leads separately from solar?

We treat solar and battery as one product, because buyers do. Every lead tells you whether they want panels, storage or both, so you know what you are selling before you call. You are not buying two separate streams.

Are battery-only leads worth buying?

They are often the strongest leads on the board. A household that already has panels has already bought from someone in your industry, already believes the maths and is usually asking because their bill or an outage pushed them. There is far less education needed.

Which states have the best battery demand?

South Australia and Western Australia lead on attach rates, because export limits and time-varying buyback rates make self-consumption the obvious move. Queensland has the biggest base of ageing systems due for a battery retrofit.

Can you book battery site visits rather than send leads?

Yes. Pay per appointment works the same way for storage. We call, qualify on bill, existing system and motivation, then book the site visit into your calendar.
