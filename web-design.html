Questions

Who owns the website?

You do. Your domain, your content, your business. Ask us to put the arrangement in writing before you start and we will.

Do I have to buy leads to get a website?

No. You can take a website on its own. Most installers start with leads and add a site once the phone is busy, but there's no requirement to bundle anything.

Is hosting and maintenance included?

Yes. Hosting, updates and ongoing maintenance are covered by the monthly fee, so the site stays online and current without you thinking about it.

What does it cost?

There's a one-off setup fee and a monthly fee covering hosting and maintenance. Get in touch and we'll send the pricing with exactly what's included.

Can you work with my existing site?

Sometimes, depending on what it's built on. Send us the link and we'll tell you honestly whether it's worth rebuilding or improving.
