Questions

What's the difference between leads and appointments?

With leads we generate and verify the contact and send it to your phone, and your team calls, qualifies and books. With appointments we do the calling and qualifying too, and you get a confirmed site visit in your calendar instead.

How quickly do you call?

Within minutes of the lead coming in, while they're still actively looking. Speed to first call is the single biggest factor in whether a solar lead converts. It's the main reason installers move across.

Do you call as my company?

Yes. We call as your business, using your name and your qualifying criteria, so the customer's experience is with you from the first contact.

What if an appointment doesn't show?

We confirm every appointment by SMS before it lands in your calendar and follow up ahead of the booking. How no-shows are treated is something we agree with you up front, before you start, and we'll put it in writing.

Can I set the criteria?

Yes. Minimum bill, postcodes, property type, timeline, whatever matters to you. We only book what fits, and we'll tell you honestly if the criteria are so tight the volume won't be there.

Can I start with leads and switch later?

Plenty of installers do exactly that. Take the leads first, see how the team handles the call volume, then move across if the phone keeps losing to the roof.
