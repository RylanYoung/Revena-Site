What makes Western Australia different

A time-varying buyback rate makes load-shifting and storage central to the pitch, so leads asking about batteries convert particularly well.

Rebates and tariffs

Federal STCs apply statewide. WA’s Distributed Energy Buyback Scheme pays a time-varying rate through Synergy and Horizon Power, which changes the self-consumption maths. Incentive programs change regularly, so confirm current eligibility and rebate values before you build a proposal around them.

Solar and battery demand in WA

Solar and battery are one enquiry now, and most WA buyers are weighing both, some want storage designed in from the start, and others already have panels and want it added. Every lead tells you which, so you know what you're selling before you call. Federal STCs apply statewide. WA’s Distributed Energy Buyback Scheme pays a time-varying rate through Synergy and Horizon Power, which changes the self-consumption maths.

Where the commercial work is

The strongest commercial segments in Western Australia tend to be mining services and fabrication through Kwinana and Welshpool, agriculture across the Wheatbelt, and cold storage around Canning Vale. These are daytime-load businesses, which is what makes the payback case work, because they consume what they generate instead of exporting it at a low buyback rate.
