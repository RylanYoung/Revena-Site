Common questions

Should I take leads or appointments?

Leads suit a business with someone who can pick up the phone within minutes and follow up properly. They're the cheaper unit and the better margin. If nobody can call that fast, appointments convert better, because we do the calling and qualifying and you get a booked site visit instead.

What counts as a qualified lead?

Three things, all of them: SMS-verified and reachable, owns the roof or can authorise the install, and genuinely after a solar and battery quote. Miss any one and it doesn't count, so you aren't charged for it. The same standard applies before we book an appointment.

Are they exclusive to me?

Yes. Every lead and every appointment is sold once, to you only. We never resell the same homeowner or business to other installers, and we cap how many providers we supply in a given market so what you get stays worth calling.

How does payment work?

Leads are bought in packs and paid upfront. Appointments are billed per booked site visit. If paying for a full pack in one go doesn't suit your cashflow, tell us. We have options that spread it out.

Do you do more than leads and appointments?

Yes. We build websites and run AI systems, including a review system, a website chatbot and an AI receptionist that answers your calls and books appointments.

How fast can I start?

Usually 24 to 48 hours. We confirm your service area and criteria, set your monthly cap, secure your spot in that market, and it goes live. If you're taking leads, speed matters after that too. The installers who call back within five minutes convert far better than the ones who wait until tomorrow.
