Questions

What counts as a qualified lead?

Three things, all of them: SMS-verified and reachable, owns the roof or can authorise the install, and genuinely after a solar and battery quote. Miss any one and it doesn't count, so you aren't charged for it.

Are the leads exclusive to me?

Yes. Every lead is sold once, to you only. We never resell the same homeowner or business to other installers, and we cap how many providers we supply in a given market so your leads stay worth calling.

Should I take leads or appointments?

Leads suit a business with someone who can pick up the phone within minutes and follow up properly. If nobody can do that reliably, appointments will convert better, because we do the calling and qualifying and you get a booked site visit instead.

How does payment work?

Leads are bought in packs and paid upfront. If paying for a full pack in one go doesn't suit your cashflow, tell us. We have options that spread it out.

How fast can I start?

Usually 24 to 48 hours. We confirm your service area and criteria, set your volume cap, secure your spot in that market, and leads start landing on your phone.
