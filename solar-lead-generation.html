Questions

Before you ask

How does solar lead generation work?

We run the campaigns that put your offer in front of homeowners and businesses actively looking at solar and battery. The enquiry gets SMS-verified, checked for property ownership and qualified on intent. If it clears all three, it goes to one installer. If it does not, you never see it and you are not charged.

How fast do solar leads arrive?

Most installers are live within 24 to 48 hours of getting set up. Once you are live, a verified lead hits your phone within seconds of clearing qualification.

What close rate should I expect on solar leads?

Across the installers we work with, close rates run between 10% and 25% depending on how fast the lead gets called, how good the follow-up is and whether the quote is competitive. Speed to first call is the single biggest factor.

Are your solar leads shared with other installers?

Never. Every lead is exclusive and sold once. Shared leads are the reason installers stop trusting lead generation, so we do not sell them.
