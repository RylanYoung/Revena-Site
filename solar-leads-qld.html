What makes Queensland different

Air-conditioning load through the wet season, plus a large base of ageing 1.5–3kW systems now due for replacement.

Rebates and tariffs

Federal STCs apply statewide, and Queensland has run its own battery support programs alongside regulated regional feed-in tariffs set by the QCA. Incentive programs change regularly, so confirm current eligibility and rebate values before you build a proposal around them.

Solar and battery demand in QLD

Solar and battery are one enquiry now, and most QLD buyers are weighing both, some want storage designed in from the start, and others already have panels and want it added. Every lead tells you which, so you know what you're selling before you call. Federal STCs apply statewide, and Queensland has run its own battery support programs alongside regulated regional feed-in tariffs set by the QCA.

Where the commercial work is

The strongest commercial segments in Queensland tend to be agriculture and packing sheds on the Darling Downs, tourism and accommodation along the coast, and mining services through Mackay and Rockhampton. These are daytime-load businesses, which is what makes the payback case work, because they consume what they generate instead of exporting it at a low buyback rate.
