Before you get in touch

Will I get pricing without a sales call?

Yes. Fill in the three questions and we'll email pricing for what you asked about. If you'd rather talk it through, say so in the notes and we'll call instead.

How and when do I pay?

Lead packs are paid upfront. If that doesn't suit your cashflow, say so in the notes and we'll talk through options that spread it out. Web design and AI systems are priced separately, so tell us which you want and we will include them in the quote.

Do I have to be a solar company?

No. Solar and battery is what we specialise in, but we work with other local service businesses too. Pick "No" on the first question and tell us what you do.

What if my market is already taken?

We cap how many installers we supply per market to keep leads exclusive. If your area is full we'll tell you straight away and put you on the list for when a spot opens.

Can I start small and scale up?

That's the normal path. Start with a smaller monthly cap, see how your team handles the call volume and what closes, then lift it once you know the numbers.
