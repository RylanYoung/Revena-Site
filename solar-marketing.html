Questions

Before you ask

What does a solar marketing agency actually do?

Most of them run ads and send you a report. We do the part that matters to an installer: generate the enquiry, verify it, qualify it, and either send it to your phone or book the site visit in your calendar. The marketing is the means, the booked job is the point.

Do I pay a monthly retainer for solar marketing?

No. There is no retainer and no lock-in contract on leads or appointments. You buy lead packs upfront, and we have cashflow-friendly options if you need them. Websites and AI systems are priced separately.

Can you handle all of my solar marketing, not just leads?

Yes. Leads and appointments are the core, and we also build the website that converts them, the review system that keeps your rating climbing, the chatbot that answers after hours and the AI receptionist that picks up when your team cannot.

Do you work with solar installers outside the capital cities?

Yes. We generate in every state and territory and in regional centres from Toowoomba to Townsville. You choose the postcodes.
