Questions

What's the difference between the AI receptionist and appointments?

The AI receptionist handles calls coming in to your business. Appointments is outbound, where our team rings the leads we generate for you, qualifies them and books the appointment.

Can I take just one?

Yes. All three work on their own. Take whichever one solves the problem you actually have, and add the others later if they'd help.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No lock-in. The monthly fee covers the running cost and support for the tool, and you can stop whenever you want.

What does it cost?

Each one is priced separately, some with a setup fee and some without. Get in touch and we'll send the pricing along with what each includes.

Will the receptionist sound like a robot?

It's set up with your business, your services and your qualifying questions. Ask us for a demo before you commit and judge it yourself rather than taking our word for it.
